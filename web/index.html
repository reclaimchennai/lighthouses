Visual reach is the shorter of luminous range and geographical range (the horizon for a 5 m eye height), cast as sight lines that stop at the first landfall. NAVTEX coverage is DGLL's stated 250 NM, over connected sea only. Hand-typed ledger positions with digit slips or seconds typed as decimal minutes were corrected against OSM, Wikidata or the Lighthouse Directory.
