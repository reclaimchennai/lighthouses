Warning Archive

Every navigational warning India's National Hydrographic Office has in force (NAVTEX, NAVAREA VIII and Temporary & Preliminary Notices to Mariners) and every one that has ended since this archive began, with when it was first and last listed.

Sources

Warnings in force: India WINS, National Hydrographic Office, read every two hours. Earlier warnings: NHO's "warnings in force" lists (NAVTEX, NAVAREA VIII). "Ended" is the first check that no longer listed the warning. Not for navigation: always use the official broadcast, NAVTEX receiver and Notices to Mariners.
