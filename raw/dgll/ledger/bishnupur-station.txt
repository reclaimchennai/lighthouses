4. Electricity

Single Phase, 230V, 50Hz Electric Mains Supply is available from WBEDCL.

 5. Water Supply

Digged well is available in the station.

 6. Mode ofApproach

The station is well connected by all weather road. The nearest railway station is Bishnupur railway
station which is approx. 2.5 Km and nearest Airport is Kolkata which is approx. 210 km from Station.

 7. Facilities Available
Post Office, Primary Health Center, Police Station and High School all are within radius of 3 KM
from Bishnupur DNC Station.
