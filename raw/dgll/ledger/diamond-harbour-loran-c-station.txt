4. Electricity

3 Phase, 440V, 50Hz Electric Mains Supply is available from WBDCEL.

5. Water Supply
Bore well water is available in the Station.

6. Mode ofApproach
The station is well connected by all weather road. The nearest Railway Station is Diamond Harbour
railway station which is approx.6 Km and nearest Airport is Kolkata which is approx. 86 km from Station.

7. Facilities Available

Post Office, Primary Health Center, Police Station and School all are within radius of 8 KM from
Diamond Harbour LORAN C Station.
