22. Mode ofApproach

District head quarter Srikakulam Town is near about 25 Km from Lighthouse and Srikakulam Road
(CHE) Railway Station is near about 40 Km from light house. Nearby Airport is Visakhapatnam
Airport which is approx. 140 km from Kalingapatnam Lighthouse.

23. Facilities Available

Branch Post Office, High School, Govt. Hospital and Police Station all are within radius of 3 km from
Kalingapatnam Lighthouse.
