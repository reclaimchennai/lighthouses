23.      Mode ofApproach

The station is well connected by all weather road. The nearest Railway Station is Paradeep which is 10
Km from Paradeep Lighthouse. Nearest Airport is Bhubaneswar which is approx. 120 km from
Paradeep LH..

24.      Facilities Available

Post Office, Primary Health Center, Police Station and High School all are within 10 KM from Sagar
Island Lighthouse.
