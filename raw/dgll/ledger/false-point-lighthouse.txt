21.      Mode ofApproach

The station is connected through water channel, it takes approx. 1.5 Hrs from Atharabanky Ghat to
Lighthouse. One has to reach the Lighthouse through Vessel. Nearest railway station is Paradeep,
which is approx.. 20 km from False point lighthouse and nearest airport is Bhubaneswar which is
approx. 170 km from Lighthouse.

22.      Facilities Available

Post Office, Primary Health Center, Police Station and High School all are within 20 KM from False
Point Lighthouse.
