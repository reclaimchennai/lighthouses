22. Mode of Approach

The station is well connected by all weather road. The nearest Railway Station is Sompeta which is
approx. 14 Km from Baruva Lighthouse. Nearest Airport is Bhubaneswar which is approx. 200 km from
Baruva LH.

23. Facilities Available

Post Office, Primary Health center, Police Station, High school and Degree College all are within
radius of 5 kms from Baruva Lighthouse.
