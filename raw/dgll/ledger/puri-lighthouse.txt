22. Mode ofApproach

The station is well connected by all weather road. The nearest Railway Station is Puri which is 6 Km
from Puri Lighthouse. Nearest Airport is Bhubaneswar which is approx. 60 km from Puri LH.

23. Facilities Available

Post Office, Primary Health Center, Police Station and Kendriya Vidyalaya all are within radius of 8
KM from Puri Lighthouse.
