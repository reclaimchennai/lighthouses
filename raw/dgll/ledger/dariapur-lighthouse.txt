21. Mode ofApproach

The station is well connected by all weather road. The nearest Railway Station is Contai which is
approx. 24 Km from Dariapur Lighthouse and nearest Airport is Kolkata which is approx. 200 km
from Lighthouse.

22. Facilities Available
Branch Post Office, High School, Hospital and Police Station all are within 22 km from Lighthouse.
