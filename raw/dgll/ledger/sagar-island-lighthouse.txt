22.     Mode ofApproach

To visit the station one has to cross the Hooglyriver through Vessels from Lot No.8 Ghat to
KachuberiaGhat. Distance from KachuberiaGhat to Lighthouse is approx. 32 Km. Nearest Railway
Station is Kakdwip which is approx. 44 Km from Lighthouse.

23.     Facilities Available
Post Office, Primary Health Center, Police Station and High School all are within 10 KM from Sagar
Island Lighthouse.
