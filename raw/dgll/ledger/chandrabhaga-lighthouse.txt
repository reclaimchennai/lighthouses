The station is well connected by all-weather road. The nearest Railway Station is Puri which is approx.
38 Km from Chandrabhaga Lighthouse.

23.    Facilities Available

Post Office, Primary Health Center, Police Station and Navodaya school all are within 7 KM from
Chandrabhaga Lighthouse.
