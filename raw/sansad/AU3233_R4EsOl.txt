3233. SHRI PRAVEEN PATEL:
      SHRI VISHWESHWAR HEGDE KAGERI:
      SHRI DILIP SAIKIA:
      SHRI RAVINDRA SHUKLA ALIAS RAVI KISHAN:

       Will the Minister of PORTS, SHIPPING AND WATERWAYS be pleased to state:
                          प न, पोत प रवहन और जलमाग मं ी

(a) the role of lighthouses beyond providing aids to Navigation along with the theme of
the Lighthouse Tourism Project;
(b) the manner in which the project aligns with the Government's vision for preserving
maritime heritage and promoting tourism; and
(c) the steps being taken by the Directorate General of Lighthouses and Lightships
(DGLL) to establish lighthouses and promote tourism along inland waterways in the
North-Eastern region, including site inspections and coordination with IWAI?

                                         ANSWER

                 MINISTER OF PORTS, SHIPPING AND WATERWAYS
                         (SHRI SARBANANDA SONOWAL)

(a) to (c)    Lighthouses primarily serve as Marine Aids to Navigation for ensuring safe
and efficient maritime navigation. In addition to this core function, the Government is
actively promoting Lighthouse Tourism which is in line with its vision of preserving India's
maritime heritage and promoting educational, cultural and tourism purposes under
Section 23 of the Marine Aids to Navigation Act, 2021. As part of this initiative, Lighthouse
Museums have been established at Chennai, Mahabalipuram, Alappuzha, Kannur,
Muttom, Dwarka and Gopnath to showcase the evolution of lighthouse technology and
India's maritime heritage. Under this initiative, tourism facilities have been developed at
75 lighthouses across the country as part of the Government's vision to preserve their
historical and architectural significance. List is attached as Annexure.
        The Directorate General of Lighthouses and Lightships (DGLL) and the Inland
Waterways Authority of India (IWAI) signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on
08.04.2025 to enhance river navigation safety and promote tourism through the
development and establishment of lighthouses on National Waterway-2 (NW-2). Joint
inspections were carried out by DGLL and IWAI to identify suitable sites for the
establishment of lighthouses along NW-2. Feasibility studies were conducted based on
the findings of the site inspections, considering navigational requirements, site
accessibility, land availability and tourism potential. Based on these feasibility studies,
four sites, namely Bogibeel, Silghat, Pandu and Biswanath Ghat along NW-2, have been
identified, and work orders have been issued for the establishment of lighthouses.
